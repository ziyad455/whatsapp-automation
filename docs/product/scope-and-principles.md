# Scope and principles

## First sellable product boundary

The first serious sellable MVP contains:

- business accounts, users, and tenant isolation;
- configurable profiles, hours, deterministic rules, and dynamic business data;
- one shared business-aware AI agent with safe tenant-scoped tools;
- Meta WhatsApp Cloud API transport;
- persistent conversations and AI, HUMAN, and PAUSED control;
- lead tracking and automatic follow-ups;
- the configuration and staff-facing dashboard surfaces needed to operate those capabilities.

Previous-customer campaigns, deeper analytics, production SaaS onboarding, commercial management, and broad integrations come after the core MVP is usable and validated.

## Stable product principles

- Build for real small-business workflows, not an abstract AI demonstration.
- The first validation goal is a working business pilot, not an enterprise platform.
- Automate useful routine work; do not automate every decision.
- Humans remain in control and their decisions override AI classifications.
- Missing or stale information is safer than an invented answer.
- Current business information matters more than model memory.
- Business policies are explicit data and deterministic rules, not hidden prompt text.
- One adaptable platform should support different business shapes without vertical-specific application branches.
- Add integrations, roles, infrastructure, and product breadth only when usage demonstrates the need.
- Preserve history through statuses and archival behavior where customer conversations depend on it.

## Intentionally postponed

The initial product does not require advanced RAG, a billing gateway, Shopify/POS/CRM connectors, microservices, Kubernetes, per-business agent deployments, a complex CRM, advanced campaign tooling, or a mobile application.

The architecture may leave clean extension points for proven future needs, but those extensions must not become speculative implementation work.
